How it works

From "does anyone know a technician?" to a ticket number.

Four steps, whether you're a resident, a worker, or barangay staff.

1

Create a free account

Sign up as a resident or register as a skilled worker in minutes.

2

Get matched

Residents post a request; it's routed to workers with the right skill.

3

Track the job

Both sides follow status — pending, accepted, working, completed.

4

Rate & build a record

Completed jobs build a public, ratable history for every worker.

For skilled workers

Your own job feed, filtered to what you actually do.

Log in and see only the requests that match your skill category and area — no more scrolling through jobs you can't take.

  • Requests filtered automatically by your specialty
  • Track jobs across Available, Pending, and Working
  • Toggle your availability so requests only reach you when free

For residents

Submit a request in minutes, follow it with a ticket number.

Describe the job once. It reaches every matching verified worker in your barangay, and you can watch its status change in your own feed.

  • Search and filter workers by skill and barangay
  • See real ratings from past completed jobs
  • Rate the job once it's done

About the program

Built for three people in the same barangay.

SkillConnect isn't a marketplace — it's a record the barangay keeps, made useful for everyone who touches it.

Residents

Find someone to trust without asking three neighbors first.

  • Search by skill and barangay
  • See ratings from past jobs
  • Submit a request in minutes

Skilled workers

Turn informal repair work into a listed, ratable profile.

  • Free registration and verification
  • Requests routed to your skill and area
  • A public record of completed jobs

Barangay & PESO staff

Run the program from one dashboard instead of loose paper records.

  • Verify and manage worker accounts
  • Analytics on demand by skill and area
  • Assign, track, and audit every request
